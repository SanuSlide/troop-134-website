About Troop 134

Founded in Folsom, California, Troop 134 has 34 years of Scouting history and is proudly chartered by American Legion Post 383. We are a Scout-led program for youth ages 11 to 17 (or age 10 with Arrow of Light or 5th grade completed) focused on outdoor survival, leadership growth, monthly camping trips, and community service.

Youth Leadership (Scout-Led Troop)

Troop 134 is run by the Scouts, for the Scouts! Through the Patrol Leaders' Council (PLC), elected youth leaders plan meetings, select campouts, and direct troop activities.

Senior Patrol Leader (SPL)

The top elected youth leader who runs troop meetings, chairs the Patrol Leaders' Council, and coordinates all troop activities.

Assistant Senior Patrol Leader (ASPL)

Fills in for the SPL during absences, oversees patrol leaders, and assists with meeting execution.

Patrol Leader (PL)

Elected by their patrol to represent fellow Scouts at PLC meetings, plan meals, and lead their patrol on campouts.

Scribe

Attends PLC meetings to keep official minutes, records attendance, and assists with administrative tasks.

Quartermaster

Manages troop gear, maintains camping equipment in the trailer, and oversees gear checkout for outdoor events.

Historian & Webmaster

Collects photos, maintains troop archives, and assists adult leaders with digital updates.

What We Do in a Year

Monthly Campouts

We camp almost every month—from snow camping in Sierra Nevada mountains to kayaking on local lakes and orienteering in regional parks.

Summer Camp

A week-long residential summer camp in Northern California where Scouts earn multiple merit badges and build lifelong memories.

High Adventure Excursions

For older Scouts, high-adventure treks include long-distance backpacking and wilderness survival.

Courts of Honor

Held quarterly to celebrate Scout achievements, rank advancements, and merit badge accomplishments.

Community Service

Eagle Scout service projects, Folsom park cleanups, and community conservation projects throughout the year.

Fundraisers

Annual Christmas Tree Recycling, popcorn sales, and camp card fundraisers help fund Scout activities.

Scout Oath & Law

The Scout Oath

"On my honor I will do my best to do my duty to God and my country and to obey the Scout Law; to help other people at all times; to keep myself physically strong, mentally awake, and morally straight."
